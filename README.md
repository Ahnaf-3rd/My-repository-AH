<br> This repo contains the datasets and the python codes used to train three ML models to test their accuracy and ability to predict the colors of non-theropod dinosaurs based on the melanosomes of the modern birds. For this test the Penguins were dropped since their melanosomes are different. The CSV named modern_bird_training.csv is used for the purpose. <br> The non-theropods include two Diplodocus specimen and two Psittacosaurus specimen. To validate the models, 8 extinct theropods have also been used. The models trained are Random Forest, SVM and QDA. Too recreate the results, download improvedresearchpaperresult.ipynb and the datasets.
<br> The extinct theropods used are *Anchiornis huxleyi*, *Archaeopteryx lithographica*, *Caudipteryx zoui*, Microraptor sp., Sinosauropteryx, Wulong sp. (ulna), Wulong sp. (ilium) and Wulong sp. (abdomen)
<br> The models only use aspect ratio of the animals to figure out their colors since melanosomes going through fossilization often lose their original shape and size. 
The result is that all the models predict the non theropods to be brown and even their aspect ratios fall inside the theropod brown region. Which can either mean that non theropods have completely different melanosome morphology or in the slightest chance, the non theropods tested were actually brown as predicted. <br> However, one thing is certain. We need more research in this area.
